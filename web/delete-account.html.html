Privacy Policy
Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Introduction

NAVJIVAN (Second Shadi) is a matchmaking application intended to help adults connect for the purpose of marriage. We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Mobile number used for account authentication.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide, such as name, age, gender, location, marital status, introduction and other matchmaking details.
  • Profile photographs or other images that you choose to upload.
  • Information you provide while communicating with other users through app features.
  • Basic technical and usage information needed to operate, secure and improve the application.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide matchmaking and profile-related features.
  • To allow users to communicate where such functionality is provided.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and investigate abuse or fraud.
  • To improve the application's performance and functionality.

4. Firebase and Service Providers

The application may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide authentication and store application data. Uploaded images may also be stored using services configured for the application. These services process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be visible to other registered users when you choose to place it in your matchmaking profile or use a communication feature. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect users, the service or our legal rights.

6. User Responsibility

Please do not publish sensitive information that is not necessary for matchmaking. You are responsible for the information and photographs you choose to share with other users. Please use appropriate caution when communicating or meeting another person.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their account credentials and avoid sharing confidential information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security. If account or data deletion functionality is provided in the application, you may use it. You may also contact us to request deletion of your account or personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

NAVJIVAN (Second Shadi) is intended for adults. We do not knowingly provide matchmaking services to children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
